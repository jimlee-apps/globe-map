Natural Earth is free for use in any type of project. The project asks users
to credit "Natural Earth" or "Made with Natural Earth". There is no
requirement to share derivatives.

GlobeMap keeps the country name and continent, lightly simplifies the shapes
(mapshaper, keep 70% of points, small shapes kept), and rounds coordinates to
4 decimal places (about 11 m) for a smaller static file. Lakes are already cut
out of this Natural Earth extract, so the ocean color shows through.
